**H2: Frequently Asked Questions**

**Q: What's the best watch gift for Dad under $200?**
A: The Orient Bambino V2 is our top pick under $200. It's an automatic dress watch that looks like it costs twice the price. For a more practical option, the Citizen Eco-Drive BM8180 is solar-powered and virtually maintenance-free. [See all watches under $200 →](/best-watches-under-200/)

**Q: Should I buy Dad an automatic or quartz watch?**
A: It depends on his lifestyle. If Dad is hands-on and appreciates craftsmanship, go automatic — he'll love the sweeping second hand and the "no battery" factor. If he's practical and just wants a watch that works, go quartz or solar. [Read our full comparison →](/blog/automatic-vs-quartz-gift/)

**Q: What size watch should I get for Dad?**
A: Most men are comfortable with 38–42mm case diameter. If you're unsure, 40mm is the universal sweet spot. You can secretly measure his current watch or use the string-around-wrist method. [Full sizing guide →](/blog/how-to-choose-watch-size/)

**Q: Is it worth engraving a watch gift?**
A: Absolutely — a caseback engraving makes any watch feel 10x more personal. Keep it short (2–3 lines max). "Happy Father's Day 2026 — Love, [Name]" never fails. [50+ engraving ideas →](/blog/watch-engraving-ideas/)

**Q: When should I order to ensure Father's Day delivery?**
A: Order at least 2 weeks before Father's Day. Amazon Prime members can usually get away with 5–7 days. If you're adding engraving, allow an extra week.

**Q: Are these watches available at local stores?**
A: Most are available on Amazon with fast shipping. Some (Tissot, Hamilton) are also available at Macy's, Nordstrom, and authorized watch dealers. We include multiple retailer options in our full reviews.
