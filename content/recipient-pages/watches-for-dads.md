## Why a Watch Is the Perfect Gift for Dad

Let's be honest: buying gifts for Dad is hard. He says he doesn't want anything. He means it. But that's exactly why a watch works — because a watch isn't just "something." It's a daily reminder that his kid (or his partner) thinks he's worth remembering.

Dads are the worst at treating themselves. They'll drive the car with the check-engine light on for six months but refuse to buy themselves a new shirt. They'll wear the same beat-up watch from 2009 because "it still works." A great watch breaks that cycle — and unlike ties, tools, or gift cards, he'll actually wear it every single day.

Here's what most gift guides miss about dads: the gift needs to match the dad. The dad who spends weekends building a deck needs a different watch than the dad who coaches soccer. The retired dad is different from the dad in the C-suite. This guide accounts for all of them.

**What makes our guide different:**

- Picks organized by dad type (practical, classic, adventurous, tough, sentimental)
- Every budget from $100 to $700
- Father's Day picks highlighted, but works for any occasion
- Engravable options for personalized keepsakes
- Gift-Worthiness Scores measure the "Dad opening the box" moment

## FAQ

### What's the best watch gift for Dad?

Our top overall pick is the **Seiko Presage SRPD37** ($280–$320). It's beautiful enough to surprise him, practical enough for daily wear, and at $300, it sits in the "meaningful without guilt" zone that dads appreciate. For Father's Day specifically, the **Citizen BM8180** ($100–$140) is the classic choice.

### How much should I spend on a watch for Dad?

Father's Day: $100–$300. Birthday: $150–$500. Retirement: $300–$700. But here's the truth about dads — most will be moved by any watch you give them, regardless of price. An engraved $130 Fossil with "Best Dad Ever" will probably make him cry just as much as a $500 Tissot.

### What if Dad says he doesn't want anything?

He's lying. Well, not lying — he genuinely believes he doesn't need anything. But the look on his face when he opens a watch box tells a different story. Get him the watch, write a card, and watch him pretend he's not getting emotional.

### My dad doesn't wear watches — should I still get one?

Many dads stopped wearing watches when smartphones arrived. A well-chosen watch can bring them back. Start with something low-maintenance (Citizen Eco-Drive — solar, no batteries) and comfortable (canvas or rubber strap). The BM8180 converts more non-watch-wearing dads than any other model.

### What's the best retirement watch for Dad?

For retirement, go premium. The **Tissot PRX Powermatic 80** ($450–$500) or **Hamilton Khaki Field** ($400–$475) are our top retirement picks. He's earned something extraordinary, and a Swiss-made watch with decades of life left is the perfect companion for the next chapter.
