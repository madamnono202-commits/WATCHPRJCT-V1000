# Casio G-Shock GA2100 "CasiOak" Review

Nicknamed the "CasiOak" for its uncanny resemblance to the $30,000+ Audemars Piguet Royal Oak, the GA2100 became the most talked-about watch under $150 on TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube. ## The Design — Why Everyone Is Talking About It

The GA2100's octagonal bezel is the design element that launched a thousand TikToks. The angular shape mirrors the Audemars Piguet Royal Oak — one of the most iconic luxury watch designs ever created. At $100 instead of $30,000, the GA2100 offers a similar visual silhouette at 0.3% of the price.

But the GA2100 isn't a knockoff. It's a G-Shock that happens to share design DNA with a luxury icon. The Carbon Core Guard construction is uniquely Casio — a carbon fiber reinforced case that's lighter and more shock-resistant than traditional resin. At 51g, the GA2100 is the thinnest, lightest G-Shock in the lineup — closer to wearing a bracelet than a brick.

The analog-digital hybrid dial combines traditional hands with a small digital display for secondary functions (day, date, seconds). This dual-display approach gives the GA2100 an analog "feel" that most G-Shocks lack — making it the G-Shock for people who don't usually like G-Shocks.

The colorway selection is massive. Black on black (stealth), all-white (clean), earth tones (outdoor), neon accents (street) — there's a GA2100 for virtually every personality. This makes it an excellent gift because you can match the color to the recipient's style.

## Toughness — The G-Shock Promise

G-Shock's core value proposition has been unchanged since 1983: this watch will not break. The GA2100 delivers:

- **200m water resistance** — swimming, surfing, diving (recreational), rain, mud, whatever
- **Shock resistance** — survives drops from height, impacts, vibrations
- **Carbon Core Guard** — carbon fiber reinforced case protects the module
- **Mineral crystal** — scratch-resistant (though not sapphire-level)

In practical terms, the GA2100 is essentially indestructible under normal use. Construction workers, athletes, military personnel, and outdoor enthusiasts have beaten G-Shocks into submission for 40 years — and the G-Shocks keep winning.

For gift-giving, this durability is a feature: you'll never hear "I broke the watch you gave me."

## On the Wrist

Despite the 45.4mm case diameter, the GA2100 wears smaller than you'd expect. The 11.8mm thickness (thin for a G-Shock) and 51g weight create a wearing experience that's closer to a sport watch than the classic G-Shock "puck on your wrist" feeling.

The resin strap is soft and pliable from day one — no break-in needed. It's comfortable for all-day wear, including sleeping and exercise. The buckle closure is simple and secure.

The one wearability issue is the digital display. It's small and can be difficult to read in low light without activating the LED backlight (accessed via a pusher at 7 o'clock). The analog hands are legible in most conditions, but the digital sub-display requires squinting.

**Comfort rating:** 8.5/10 — light, soft, virtually disappears on the wrist.

The "full metal CasiOak" — a GA2100 with aftermarket steel bracelet and bezel — has become its own sub-category. Total cost: $130–$220 for a watch + mod kit combination that turns heads and starts conversations.

## FAQ

### Is the Casio G-Shock GA2100 worth it?

Yes. For $90–$120, you get 200m WR, shock resistance, iconic design, and social-media cachet. It's the best casual/sport watch under $150.

### Why is it called the CasiOak?

The nickname combines "Casio" and "Royal Oak" — the GA2100's octagonal bezel resembles the Audemars Piguet Royal Oak, a $30,000+ luxury icon. The GA2100 delivers a similar visual silhouette for $100.

### Can you swim with the G-Shock GA2100?

Absolutely. 200m (20 ATM) water resistance handles swimming, surfing, snorkeling, and recreational diving. It's one of the toughest water-resistant watches at any price.

### Is the G-Shock GA2100 good for small wrists?

Despite the 45.4mm case, it wears well on wrists 6.5" and up thanks to the 11.8mm thickness and 51g weight. Under 6.5", the smaller GA-2100S or GM-S2100 (40.4mm) are better options.

### What's the best GA2100 color?

The all-black GA-2100-1A1 is the most popular and versatile. Earth tones (GA-2100-5A) are great for outdoor enthusiasts. White (GA-2100-7A) is clean and minimal. Choose based on the recipient's wardrobe.
