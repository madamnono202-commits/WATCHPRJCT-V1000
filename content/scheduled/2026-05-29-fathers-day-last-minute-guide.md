## Last-Minute Gifting Tips

1. **Add a handwritten note.** A 2-sentence card transforms a "late" gift into a thoughtful one. "Dad — every second on this watch is a reminder of how much you mean to me."

2. **Use Amazon gift wrapping.** It's a few dollars extra and saves the stress of wrapping yourself. Select "Add gift options" at checkout.

3. **Include a gift receipt.** Shows thoughtfulness, not uncertainty.

4. **Don't apologize for "lateness."** He doesn't know when you ordered it. The gift speaks for itself.

## FAQ

### How late can I order for Father's Day delivery?

With Amazon Prime, typically Wednesday or Thursday of Father's Day week for Saturday/Sunday delivery. Check the specific delivery estimate at checkout.

### Is it obvious if I bought a last-minute watch gift?

Not at all. These watches arrive in manufacturer presentation boxes with proper packaging. There's no "I bought this yesterday" tell. The quality speaks for itself.

### What if it arrives after Father's Day?

A great watch arriving a day late is still better than a mediocre gift on time. Tell him it's coming — the anticipation is part of the experience.
