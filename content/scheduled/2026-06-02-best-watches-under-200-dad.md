## Why Under $200 Is Dad's Sweet Spot

<!-- ~150 words -->

There's a reason $200 is the most popular budget for Father's Day watches: it's generous enough to buy genuine quality, but sensible enough that nobody feels awkward. At this price point, you get automatic movements from Orient and Seiko, solar technology from Citizen, Swiss heritage from Tissot, and bombproof durability from Casio.

These aren't "budget" watches in the pejorative sense. They're watches that happen to cost under $200 while delivering quality, style, and reliability that rivals pieces at twice the price. Dad won't know (or care) what you spent — he'll care that you chose something perfect for him.

### 1. Orient Bambino V2 — $130–$170

**Movement:** Automatic · **Gift Score:** 9.1/10

The [Orient Bambino V2](/reviews/orient-bambino-v2/) is the most impressive watch you can buy for Dad under $200 — and it's not even close. The domed mineral crystal, cream sunburst dial, and blue dauphine hands create a watch that looks like it belongs in a $500 display case. The in-house automatic movement with exhibition caseback gives him something to show off at dinner.

**Best for:** Dads who dress well, date-night dads, dads who appreciate aesthetics

**Pros:**
- Looks 3x its price — the perceived value gap is enormous
- In-house automatic movement with exhibition caseback
- Domed crystal creates vintage-luxury depth
- Available in multiple dial/strap combinations

**Cons:**
- Only 30m water resistance — no swimming
- Leather strap needs break-in time
- No hacking (seconds hand doesn't stop when setting time)
