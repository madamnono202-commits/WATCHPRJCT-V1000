## What to Look for in a Graduation Watch

<!-- ~300 words -->

### Versatility Is Everything

His life is about to change — new job, new city, new social circles. His graduation watch needs to work in all of them. Look for clean designs in neutral colors (silver, blue, black, navy) that bridge casual and professional settings.

### Quality He Can Grow Into

This isn't a fashion watch that'll feel disposable in six months. Look for:
- **Stainless steel cases** — durable, substantial, timeless
- **Sapphire or hardlex crystals** — scratch-resistant for daily wear
- **Reputable movements** — automatic or solar, from established caliber families
- **Brand heritage** — Seiko, Hamilton, Tissot, Orient, Citizen

### The Right Size

The current sweet spot is 38–42mm. This range flatters most wrists and reads as intentional without being oversized. [Our full sizing guide →](/blog/how-to-choose-watch-size/)

### Presentation Matters

Graduation is ceremonial. The unboxing should match the moment. Brands like Seiko, Tissot, and Hamilton ship in presentation boxes that elevate the experience. Avoid brands where the packaging is an afterthought.

### Budget Guidelines

| Milestone | Suggested Budget | Sweet Spot |
|-----------|-----------------|------------|
| High School Graduation | $75–$150 | Citizen, Casio, Orient |
| College Graduation | $150–$350 | Seiko, Tissot, Hamilton |
| Grad School / MBA | $300–$500+ | Hamilton, Tissot, Longines |

## FAQ

### Is a watch a good graduation gift?

A great watch is one of the best graduation gifts you can give. It's personal, lasting, and marks the occasion in a tangible way. Unlike electronics that become obsolete, a quality watch becomes more meaningful over time.

### How much should I spend on a graduation watch?

For high school: $75–$150. For college: $150–$350. For grad school: $300–$500+. These ranges deliver genuine quality without overextending.

### Should I get an automatic or quartz graduation watch?

For watch enthusiasts or appreciators of craftsmanship: automatic. For practical, low-maintenance guys: solar/quartz. When in doubt, automatic — the mechanical movement adds narrative depth that makes the gift more memorable.

### When should I give a graduation watch?

At the graduation dinner or a private moment — not at the ceremony itself. Give him space to appreciate it, try it on, and react without an audience of hundreds.
